# Glossary

Terms used across gata's code, issues, and docs. Use these names, not synonyms.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Trip** | A multi-day trek with camping, planned by a small team. |
| **Organiser** | The one member who created the trip. Only they can invite people, remove members, or delete the trip, and they must hand over the role before leaving. |
| **Member** | A user on a trip. Every member can edit the plan. |
| **Invite** | A single-use, 7-day link for one email address to join one trip. The organiser can cancel it. |
| **Day** | One entry in a trip's ordered list of days. Either a walking day or a rest day. |
| **Walking day** | A day with hand-entered distance (km), ascent (m), and optional descent (m), ending at a camp. |
| **Rest day** | A day with no distance that stays at the previous camp. |
| **Camp** | A named overnight spot with a map position and optional notes. |
| **Gear library** | A user's own reusable list of kit with weights in grams. It follows them from trip to trip. |
| **Trip gear item** | A piece of kit on a trip. Its name and weight are copied from the gear library when it's added. It is either personal or shared. |
| **Shared item** | Kit used by the group, such as a tent or stove. It has exactly one carrier. |
| **Carrier** | The member who carries a shared item or a meal. |
| **Needs carrier** | A shared item or meal whose carrier left the trip. |
| **Meal** | Breakfast, lunch, dinner, or a snack on a given day, with weight, kcal, and a carrier. |
| **Pack weight** | Worked out from the data, not stored: a member's personal items, plus the shared items and meals they carry. |
| **Emergency contacts** | One or two contacts (name, relationship, phone) on a user's profile, shown to their trip mates and on the trip sheet. |
| **Emergency notes** | Optional free text on a trip, such as the expected return time and who to call. |
| **Trip sheet** | A print-friendly page for a trip, saved as a PDF from the browser. |
| **Version** | A number on each editable row, used to warn when you save over a newer edit. |
